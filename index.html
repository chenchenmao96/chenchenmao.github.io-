Readable but Less Faithful

Source-Text Complexity, Trust, and Disclosure Willingness in AI Translation

How does the way an AI renders an answer change what people think the system understands—and what they are willing to share with it? I designed and ran a 2×2 experiment using TransLingo, a cue-free translation interface built specifically for the study.

Question

How do readability, fidelity, and source complexity shape perceived quality?

Method

Prolific recruitment, stimulus audit, and structural equation modeling in R.

Signal

Perceived quality was associated with intelligence, anthropomorphism, trust, and disclosure.

About

I study what AI capabilities come to mean in the minds of the people using them.

I’m a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at Lehigh University working across human–AI interaction, social computing, and behavioral science. My research asks how people interpret AI capabilities and output characteristics, when those interpretations become perceived intelligence or anthropomorphism, and how they relate to trust and willingness to disclose.

I’m especially interested in research roles where I can investigate these human responses in close collaboration with AI research and evaluation teams.
