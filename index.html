Selected research

01

AI translation

2×2 experiment · N = 306

Readable but Less Faithful

Source-Text Complexity, Trust, and Disclosure Willingness in AI Translation

I built TransLingo, a plain-text translation interface without an avatar, voice, or persona, to isolate how readability- and fidelity-oriented renderings affect perceived translation quality. The study also examined how source-text complexity changes that evaluation.

Analysis
Stimulus-level audit, behavioral experiment, and structural equation modeling in R.
What emerged
Perceived quality was associated with perceived intelligence, anthropomorphism, trust, and willingness to disclose.
02

Social computing

Research infrastructure

Outer Limits

An Experimental Approach to Controlled Content Manipulation within the Reddit Interface

Outer Limits is a browser-based research tool that changes live Reddit content and engagement cues while preserving the platform’s familiar interface. It supports controlled experiments without asking participants to imagine how a social platform might feel.

Chrome extension · Hybrid field–lab method · Ecological realism

Completed field experiment

From Tool to Social Actor

How LLM Capabilities and Social Context Shape Trust

A 2×2 experiment deployed through Outer Limits tested whether multifunctionality and explanation change how people understand a general-purpose AI—as a functional tool or as something closer to a social actor.

Perceived intelligence · Anthropomorphism · AI literacy · Benevolence and integrity trust

About

I study what AI comes to mean in the minds of the people using it.

I’m a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at Lehigh University working across human–AI interaction, social computing, and behavioral science. My research asks how people interpret AI capabilities, output characteristics, and social context; when those interpretations become perceived intelligence or anthropomorphism; and how they relate to trust and willingness to disclose.

I specialize in human-centered evaluation of AI systems and am especially interested in research roles where I can investigate these human responses alongside AI research and evaluation teams.
